STRUCTURAL ANALYSIS OF BOX CULVERT

INPUTS

1. GEOMETRY

Top width of wall \(w_t\)
Bottom width of wall \(w_b\)
Thickness of top slab \(t_s\)
Thickness of bottom slab \(t_b\)
Thickness of haunch \(w_h\)
Inner width \(B\)
Inner height \(H\)
Depth of water inside \(h_w\)
Depth of silt inside \(h_{silt}\)

2. GEOTECHNICAL DATA

2.1 FILL MATERIAL

Angle of repose \(\phi_s\)
Inclination of fill material \(i_s\)
Soil depth above top slab \(h_s\)
Water table above bottom of culvert \(h_{wt}\)
Dry density of soil \(\gamma_d\)
Saturated density of soil \(\gamma_{sat}\)

2.2 FOUNDATION MATERIAL

Anlge of repose \(\phi_f\)
Cohesion \(c_f\)

OTHERS

% of passive pressure consideration \(C_a\)
% of hydrostatic pressure during saturation \(W_w\)

At rest condition has been considered

LOAD CALCULATION

1. For self-weight of the structure, area of section shall be calculated.

Fig. Box Culvert

Area of top slab

\(A_1=B×t_s\)

Area of wall

\(A_2=2×\frac{1}{2}(H+t_s+t_b)(w_t+w_b)\)

Area of base slab

\(A_3=B×t_b\)

Area of haunch

\(A_4=\frac{1}{2}w_h^2\)

Total Area of culvert, \(A\)

\(A=A_1+A_2+A_3+4A_4\)

Centroid of the section,\(\bar{x}\)

\(\bar{x}=\frac{(B+2w_b)}{2}\)
